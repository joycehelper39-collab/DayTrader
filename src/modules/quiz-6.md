# Module 6 Quiz: Supply and Demand Zones

**1.** What creates supply and demand zones?
- A) Random price movements
- B) Institutional orders creating price imbalances
- C) News events only
- D) Technical indicators

**2.** A demand zone forms when:
- A) Price drops sharply from a consolidation
- B) Price explodes upward from a consolidation with large candles
- C) Price moves sideways indefinitely
- D) Volume is low

**3.** What is a "base" in supply/demand terminology?
- A) The bottom of a chart
- B) The consolidation area before the explosive move
- C) The stop loss level
- D) The take profit level

**4.** When price returns to a demand zone, you should expect:
- A) Immediate continued drop
- B) Buyers to defend the zone
- C) No reaction
- D) Volume to disappear

**5.** How do supply and demand zones differ from support/resistance?
- A) They are the same thing
- B) S/D zones focus on where institutional orders created imbalances
- C) S/D zones are always horizontal
- D) Support/resistance is more accurate

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Institutional orders create price imbalances that form zones
2. **B** - Demand zone forms when price explodes upward from consolidation
3. **B** - The base is the consolidation area before the explosive move
4. **B** - Buyers defend demand zones when price returns
5. **B** - S/D zones focus on institutional order flow imbalances
