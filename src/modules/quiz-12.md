# Module 12 Quiz: Building a Trading Plan

**1.** A trading plan should be:
- Flexible and changing
- Written down and followed
- Based on emotions
- Simple notes

**2.** Your trading plan should define:
- Only entry rules
- Only exit rules
- Every aspect of how and why you trade
- Just your strategy

**3.** Best trading sessions for US stocks are:
- Pre-market only
- First and last hour
- Lunch only
- After hours only

**4.** A complete plan should include:
- Entry criteria only
- Exit criteria only
- Entry, exit, risk management, and journaling
- Just market selection

**5.** Your plan should be based on:
- Gut feeling
- Backtested rules
- Random selection
- Other traders' opinions

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Must be written and followed consistently
2. **C** - Complete plan covers every aspect
3. **B** - First hour (9:30-10:30) and last hour have highest volume
4. **C** - Entry, exit, risk management, journal requirements
5. **B** - Plan should be based on backtested rules
