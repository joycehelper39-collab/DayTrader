# Module 2 Quiz: Market Structure

**1.** In an uptrend, what defines the trend?
- A) Lower highs and lower lows
- B) Higher highs and higher lows
- C) Flat price action
- D) High volatility

**2.** What is a "break of structure" (BOS)?
- A) Price breaks a key support level
- B) Price breaks the previous swing low in an uptrend
- C) A trading session ends
- D) An indicator gives a signal

**3.** What is required to validate a swing low?
- A) It must be the lowest point ever
- B) Price must subsequently break the previous swing high
- C) It must have high volume
- D) It must be on a weekly chart

**4.** What does the cardinal rule of trading with market structure state?
- A) Trade in both directions for balance
- B) Only trade in the direction of the prevailing trend
- C) Always wait for 100% confirmation
- D) Only trade at support and resistance

**5.** Which timeframe should determine your primary trend?
- A) 1-minute chart
- B) 5-minute chart
- C) Daily or 4-hour chart
- D) 1-hour chart

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Higher highs (HH) and higher lows (HL) define an uptrend
2. **B** - A BOS occurs when price breaks the previous swing low in an uptrend
3. **B** - A swing low is valid when price breaks the previous swing high
4. **B** - Only trade in the direction of the prevailing trend
5. **C** - Higher timeframes (Daily/4H) define the primary trend
