# Module 15 Quiz: Broker Selection

**1.** Key factor for day trading brokers:
- Low interest rates
- Fast execution speed
- Crypto only
- No mobile app

**2.** For US stocks, watch for:
- PDT rule (Pattern Day Trader)
- No trading hours
- High commissions
- Limited stocks

**3.** Hidden fees to watch for:
- Commission only
- Withdrawal, inactivity, data fees
- Free everything
- One-time setup fee

**4.** Regulation is important because:
- It doesn't matter
- Unregulated brokers can vanish
- Regulated = guaranteed profits
- Only for large accounts

**5.** Paper trading accounts are:
- Useless
- Good for practice before live trading
- Only for professionals
- Illegal

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Fast execution is critical for day trading
2. **A** - PDT rule affects accounts under $25,000
3. **B** - Watch for hidden fees: withdrawal, inactivity, data
4. **B** - Unregulated brokers can disappear with funds
5. **B** - Paper trading essential before risking real money
