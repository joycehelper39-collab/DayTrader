Here is a structured daily routine that incorporates everything from this course.[^36]

### Pre-Market (30-60 Minutes Before Session Open)

1. **Check the economic calendar**: Identify any high-impact news releases (NFP, CPI, FOMC, GDP). Avoid trading during these events as a beginner.
2. **Review higher timeframe charts (Daily/4H)**: Determine the trend direction and mark major supply/demand zones on each of your instruments.
3. **Build your watchlist**: Identify 2-4 instruments that are approaching key zones with clear setups developing.
4. **Set price alerts**: Place alerts at the key zones so you're notified when price arrives.
5. **Review your trading plan rules**: Read them before the session to prime your execution.

### During the Session

6. **Wait for price to reach your zones**: Do not force trades. If nothing is at your zones, do nothing.
7. **Confirm with lower timeframe**: When price reaches a zone, switch to your execution timeframe and look for candlestick confirmation.[^16]
8. **Execute according to your plan**: Enter, set stop-loss, set take-profit. Then step back.
9. **Do not move your stop-loss** (unless trailing it in profit according to your rules).
10. **Manage your emotions**: If you hit your max daily loss, close the platform and walk away.

### Post-Market (15-30 Minutes After Session Close)

11. **Journal every trade**: Record all the fields from Module 14.
12. **Take screenshots**: Save chart images of your setups.
13. **Rate your execution**: Did you follow the plan? Score yourself 1-10.
14. **Identify improvements**: Write one thing you'll do better tomorrow.
