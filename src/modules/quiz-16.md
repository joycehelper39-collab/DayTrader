# Module 16 Quiz: Daily Routine

**1.** Pre-market should include:
- Trading without analysis
- Checking economic calendar, reviewing charts, building watchlist
- Sleeping
- Reacting to overnight moves

**2.** During market hours, you should:
- Force trades if nothing is at your zones
- Wait for price to reach your zones
- Trade every candle
- Ignore your plan

**3.** Max daily loss rule means:
- Keep trading after losses
- Stop trading when you hit your limit
- Increase position size
- Switch strategies

**4.** Post-market review should happen:
- Before trading
- 15-30 minutes after close
- During trading hours
- Never

**5.** Setting price alerts helps:
- Force you to watch screens constantly
- Get notified when price reaches your zones
- Increase screen time
- Avoid planning

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Pre-market: calendar, charts, watchlist, alerts
2. **B** - Wait for price at YOUR zones, don't force trades
3. **B** - Stop trading when max daily loss hit
4. **B** - Post-market: 15-30 min after close for review
5. **B** - Alerts notify you when price reaches zones
