# Module 11 Quiz: Trading Psychology

**1.** What causes most traders to fail?
- Poor strategy
- Poor psychology
- Bad luck
- High fees

**2.** FOMO stands for:
- Fear Of Making Options
- Fear Of Missing Out
- Finding Optimal Market Orders
- Following Only Momentum

**3.** Revenge trading usually leads to:
- Quick recovery
- Larger losses
- Break even
- Learning experience

**4.** Mark Douglas taught traders to:
- Think in certainties
- Think in probabilities
- Avoid all risk
- Trade emotionally

**5.** After a losing trade, you should:
- Immediately take another trade
- Step away and reset
- Increase position size
- Ignore the loss

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Most fail due to psychology, not strategy
2. **B** - FOMO = Fear Of Missing Out
3. **B** - Revenge trading almost always leads to larger losses
4. **B** - Think in probabilities, not certainties
5. **B** - Step away after losses, don't revenge trade
