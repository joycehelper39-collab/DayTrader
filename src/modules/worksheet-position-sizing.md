# Position Size Calculator Worksheet

Use this to calculate exactly how many shares/units/lots to trade.

---

## Your Parameters

| Input | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Account Size | $__________ |
| Risk Per Trade | _______% |
| Dollar Risk | $__________ |
| Entry Price | __________ |
| Stop Loss Price | __________ |
| Risk Per Share | $__________ |

---

## Calculation

```
Position Size = Dollar Risk ÷ Risk Per Share
```

**Your Position Size:** __________ shares/units

---

## Example

| Account | Risk % | $ Risk | Entry | Stop | Risk/Share | Position |
|---------|--------|--------|-------|------|------------|----------|
| $10,000 | 1% | $100 | $50.00 | $49.00 | $1.00 | 100 shares |
| $10,000 | 2% | $200 | $50.00 | $49.00 | $1.00 | 200 shares |
| $5,000 | 1% | $50 | 1.0500 | 1.0400 | 0.0100 | 5,000 units |

---

## Forex Pip Calculation

```
Position Size (lots) = Dollar Risk ÷ (Pip Value × Stop in Pips)
```

| Account | Risk % | $ Risk | Pair | Stop (pips) | Pip Value | Lots |
|---------|--------|--------|------|-------------|-----------|------|
| $10,000 | 1% | $100 | EUR/USD | 20 | $10 | 0.5 lots |
| $10,000 | 2% | $200 | GBP/USD | 30 | $10 | 0.67 lots |

---

## Quick Reference: Risk to Position Size

| If Risk/Share Is... | And Dollar Risk Is... | Position Size Is... |
|---------------------|----------------------|---------------------|
| $0.50 | $100 | 200 shares |
| $1.00 | $100 | 100 shares |
| $2.00 | $100 | 50 shares |
| $5.00 | $100 | 20 shares |
| $10.00 | $100 | 10 shares |

---

## Common Mistake

**Never** adjust your stop loss to fit a desired position size.  
**Always** calculate position size based on your fixed risk amount.
