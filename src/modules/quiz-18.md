# Module 18 Quiz: Common Mistakes

**1.** Trading without a plan leads to:
- Consistency
- Emotional decisions
- Better results
- More confidence

**2.** Moving stop-losses to prevent losses usually:
- Works
- Increases losses
- Has no effect
- Guarantees profits

**3.** Going live too early without backtesting:
- Is smart
- Leads to losses
- Guarantees success
- Doesn't matter

**4.** Overtrading is caused by:
- Patience
- Boredom or FOMO
- Good planning
- Low risk

**5.** The biggest mistake beginners make is:
- Using too much analysis
- Focusing on risk management
- Ignoring risk management
- Trading too small

## Answer Key

1. **B** - No plan = emotional, inconsistent trading
2. **B** - Moving stops usually increases losses
3. **B** - Skipping backtesting leads to losses
4. **B** - Overtrading comes from boredom/FOMO
5. **C** - Ignoring risk management is the biggest mistake
