# Module 17 Quiz: Scaling Your Account

**1.** Compounding works by:
- Taking increasingly larger risks
- Reinvesting profits to grow capital
- Adding more money from outside
- Avoiding losses

**2.** In Stage 1 (learning), risk per trade should be:
- 5-10%
- 0.5-1%
- 20%
- As high as possible

**3.** Before increasing position size, you should be:
- On a losing streak
- Consistently profitable
- Using maximum leverage
- Trading emotionally

**4.** Scaling up too fast leads to:
- Guaranteed success
- Blowing up your account
- Lower stress
- Better sleep

**5.** The key to account growth is:
- High risk tolerance
- Consistency and capital preservation
- Large positions
- Ignoring risk management

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Reinvesting profits compounds growth
2. **B** - Learning stage: 0.5-1% risk per trade
3. **B** - Only increase size when consistently profitable
4. **B** - Scaling too fast = blown account
5. **B** - Consistency and preserving capital enable growth
