# Module 14 Quiz: Trading Journal

**1.** A trading journal is:
- Optional for profitable traders
- The most important tool for improvement
- Only for beginners
- A waste of time

**2.** Every trade should record:
- Just the P&L
- Entry, exit, setup, P&L, screenshot, notes
- Only the entry price
- Just the direction

**3.** Weekly review helps identify:
- Which trades to take
- Patterns in your mistakes
- When to skip trading
- All of the above

**4.** Without a journal, you cannot:
- Identify patterns in mistakes
- Improve over time
- Both A and B
- Trade profitably

**5.** A journal screenshot should capture:
- Just the entry
- Just the exit
- The setup and entry
- Nothing important

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Journal is the most important improvement tool
2. **B** - Record everything: entry, exit, setup, P&L, screenshot, notes
3. **D** - Weekly review shows patterns, when to skip, what works
4. **C** - Without journal, can't identify mistakes or improve
5. **C** - Screenshot should show the setup AND entry
