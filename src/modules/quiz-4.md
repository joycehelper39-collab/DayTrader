# Module 4 Quiz: Chart Patterns

**1.** A head and shoulders pattern signals:
- A) Continuation of uptrend
- B) Bearish reversal
- C) Bullish continuation
- D) Sideways market

**2.** A bull flag forms after:
- A) A strong downward move
- B) A strong upward move
- C) A gap up
- D) High volatility

**3.** In a double top, the "trigger line" is:
- A) The highest peak
- B) The lowest trough between peaks
- C) The 50-day moving average
- D) The volume average

**4.** An ascending triangle suggests:
- A) Bearish breakdown
- B) Bullish breakout above resistance
- C) No predictable direction
- D) Range-bound only

**5.** The target for a head and shoulders is:
- A) The height of the head
- B) Distance from head to neckline projected down
- C) The previous swing low
- D) 1:1 risk reward

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Head and shoulders signals bearish reversal
2. **B** - Bull flag forms after strong upward move (flagpole)
3. **B** - Trigger line is the low between the two peaks
4. **B** - Ascending triangle typically breaks out bullish
5. **B** - Target = distance from head to neckline, projected down
