# Module 10 Quiz: Risk Management

**1.** The 1-2% rule means:
- Risk 1-2% of account per trade
- Risk 1-2% per day
- Risk 1-2% per week
- Risk 1-2% per month

**2.** With $10,000 account and 2% risk, you risk:
- $100
- $200
- $500
- $1,000

**3.** Position sizing formula is:
- Account × Risk %
- Dollar Risk ÷ Risk Per Share
- (Account × Risk%) / Stop Size
- Stop Size × Account

**4.** Never adjust your stop loss to:
- Fit a larger position size
- Let a trade work longer
- Trail profits
- Reduce risk

**5.** Risk management is:
- Optional for experienced traders
- More important than strategy
- Less important than entries
- Only for large accounts

## Answer Key

1. **A** - Risk 1-2% of account per single trade
2. **B** - $10,000 × 2% = $200
3. **B** - Position Size = Dollar Risk ÷ Risk Per Share
4. **A** - Never widen stop to fit bigger position
5. **B** - Risk management is MORE important than strategy
