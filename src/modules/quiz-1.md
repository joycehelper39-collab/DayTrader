# Module 1 Quiz: What Is Day Trading?

## Questions

**1.** What distinguishes day trading from swing trading?
- A) Day trading involves higher risk
- B) Day trading closes all positions before market close
- C) Day trading requires larger capital
- D) Day trading only trades forex

**2.** Which market has the highest daily trading volume?
- A) US Stocks
- B) Cryptocurrency
- C) Forex
- D) Futures

**3.** What is scalping?
- A) Trading based on scalping patterns
- B) Making many small trades targeting small price movements
- C) Holding positions overnight for small gains
- D) Trading only during lunch hours

**4.** Which is NOT a reason people day trade?
- A) Independence
- B) No overnight risk
- C) Guaranteed profits
- D) Ability to compound small gains

**5.** What is momentum trading?
- A) Trading based on indicator momentum
- B) Riding strong price moves driven by volume and catalysts
- C) Trading in a sideways market
- D) Only trading during volatile news events

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Day trading closes all positions before market close, while swing trading holds overnight
2. **C** - Forex has the highest daily trading volume globally
3. **B** - Scalping targets small price movements with short holding times
4. **C** - Day trading does NOT guarantee profits
5. **B** - Momentum trading rides strong price moves driven by volume and catalysts
