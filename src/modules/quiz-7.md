# Module 7 Quiz: Technical Indicators

**1.** Which moving average gives more weight to recent prices?
- SMA
- EMA
- VWAP
- RSI

**2.** The 200 SMA is primarily used for:
- Scalping entries
- Identifying long-term trend
- Day trading only
- Volume analysis

**3.** When the 9 EMA crosses above the 20 EMA, this is called:
- Death cross
- Golden cross
- Flat line
- Zero cross

**4.** VWAP stands for:
- Volume Weighted Average Price
- Very Weak Average Pattern
- Vertical Weighted Analysis Price
- Volume Wave Analysis Point

**5.** Indicators primarily:
- Lead price action
- Lag behind price action
- Predict the future
- Replace price charts

## Answer Key

1. **B** - EMA gives more weight to recent prices
2. **B** - 200 SMA identifies long-term trend
3. **B** - Golden cross = shorter MA crosses above longer MA
4. **A** - VWAP = Volume Weighted Average Price
5. **B** - Indicators lag behind price action
