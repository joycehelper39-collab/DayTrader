# Module 3 Quiz: Candlestick Fundamentals

**1.** What does the body of a candlestick represent?
- A) The high and low of the period
- B) The open and close prices
- C) The trading volume
- D) The market sentiment

**2.** A green/white candle means:
- A) The close was higher than the open
- B) The close was lower than the open
- C) High volume
- D) Low volatility

**3.** What is a doji?
- A) A candle with no wicks
- B) A candle with virtually no body
- C) A very large candle
- D) A gap in price

**4.** A hammer appears at the:
- Top of an uptrend
- Bottom of a downtrend
- Middle of a trend
- After a gap

**5.** Long wicks on both sides of a small body indicate:
- Strong trend continuation
- Indecision between buyers and sellers
- High volume
- Guaranteed reversal

## Answer Key

1. **B** - The body shows open and close prices
2. **A** - Green/white = close > open (bullish)
3. **B** - Doji has tiny body, long wicks = indecision
4. **B** - Hammer forms at bottom of downtrend, signals reversal
5. **B** - Long wicks both sides = buyers and sellers fighting
