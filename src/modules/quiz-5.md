# Module 5 Quiz: Support & Resistance

**1.** A support level is where:
- A) Selling pressure prevents further decline
- B) Buying pressure prevents further decline
- C) Price always reverses immediately
- D) Volume is highest

**2.** The more times price tests a support level:
- A) The stronger it becomes
- B) The weaker it becomes
- C) The more likely it will never break
- D) It has no effect

**3.** Psychological levels often occur at:
- A) Random prices
- B) Round numbers (50, 100, 1.3000)
- C) Fibonacci numbers only
- D) Volume nodes

**4.** When support breaks, it often becomes:
- A) Stronger support
- B) Resistance
- C) Irrelevant
- D) A new support

**5.** For drawing support/resistance, you should use:
- Exact lines
- Zones/areas
- Indicator levels only
- Random points

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Support is where buying pressure prevents further decline
2. **B** - Each test weakens the level (each touch weakens)
3. **B** - Round numbers act as psychological levels
4. **B** - Broken support becomes resistance (and vice versa)
5. **B** - Use zones, not exact lines
