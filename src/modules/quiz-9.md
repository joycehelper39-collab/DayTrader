# Module 9 Quiz: Order Types

**1.** A market order:
- Executes at a specific price
- Executes immediately at best available price
- May never fill
- Is only for limit orders

**2.** A buy limit order is placed:
- Above current price
- Below current price
- At market price
- At stop price

**3.** A stop-loss order becomes a market order:
- Immediately when placed
- When stop price is reached
- At end of day
- Never

**4.** Slippage is most likely in:
- Highly liquid markets
- Fast-moving markets
- Limit orders
- Low volume afterhours

**5.** A sell stop is used to:
- Enter a long position
- Limit losses on long positions
- Enter on breakout
- Set a take profit

## Answer Key

1. **B** - Market order executes immediately at best price
2. **B** - Buy limit placed below current price
3. **B** - Stop order triggers when stop price reached
4. **B** - Slippage likely in fast-moving/volatile markets
5. **B** - Sell stop limits losses on long positions
